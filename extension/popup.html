Before enabling

Once connected, Chrome routes HTTP and encrypted HTTPS connections through a local companion even when bandwidth limiting is OFF. Use Disconnect proxy to restore prior proxy settings. Website addresses and network traffic are processed on this PC only. No browsing logs, accounts or telemetry. HTTPS is not decrypted. Do not use with a required corporate proxy or VPN unless compatibility has been verified.

Installation & troubleshooting

Windows companion required. OFF removes the cap but keeps the local proxy connected. Disconnect proxy restores prior settings and stops the engine. Some traffic can bypass the proxy. This is not a VPN or firewall.

Connect local proxy?

Existing direct connections and downloads may bypass the proxy until restarted. Once connected, switching the cap ON or OFF does not require reconnecting proxied downloads.

Use Disconnect proxy to completely stop the companion and restore prior proxy settings.

Only enable for a trusted VPN or DNS filter that uses synthetic addresses. Allows DNS-resolved web destinations in 198.18.0.0/15 and the specific NordVPN Google address 192.0.0.88 for google.com and its subdomains. Other private/reserved destinations stay blocked. No DNS or VPN settings are changed. Disconnect and reconnect to change this option.
